Ohio recorder uploads

Privacy policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Ohio recorder uploads is a personal, single-operator archive utility. This notice covers its use of Google Drive through rclone; it is not a public service.

Access and use

The utility accesses selected archive files and Drive metadata such as names, folder locations, sizes and checksums. It uploads recorder files, verifies stored copies, and reads or downloads archives for recovery. Full Drive OAuth permission can technically read, create, edit and delete Drive files, including existing archives; operational jobs use configured archive locations. The utility does not request Gmail, Contacts or Calendar access.

Storage and disclosure

Archive files are stored in the operator's Google Drive and may be retained on operator-controlled computers or servers for backup and integrity checks. OAuth tokens and client credentials are stored in restricted local configuration files, not on this public site. Google processes Drive data to provide its service; operator-selected hosting providers process copies stored on their infrastructure. This utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties for their independent purposes.

Limited use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data accessed through this utility is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and control

The operator controls archive retention and can delete stored files through Google Drive or the relevant local storage. Removing the app's access in Google Account third-party connections stops future authorized access; it does not automatically delete existing backups. Unneeded credentials and backup copies can be removed by the operator.

This website and contact

This static site has no app sign-in, forms, analytics scripts or app cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process ordinary web-request information under GitHub's privacy statement. For questions, contact the repository owner through GitHub issues; do not post private files, tokens or credentials publicly.

This notice will be updated if the utility's data practices change.
